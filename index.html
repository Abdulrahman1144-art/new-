What we did

From raw workbook to a reproducible baseline and an interactive local dashboard.

2

Derive BMI, keep missing comorbidity as Unknown, and exclude identifiers and later-recorded fields.

3

Explore

Use a Streamlit dashboard locally to filter and review the supplied dataset.

Data quality and limits

Cleaning can handle formatting and some missing values; it cannot verify where the workbook came from.

Handled in preparation

80,660 missing comorbidity values became Unknown. Death date and cause fields each had 171,300 missing values and were kept missing because absence is meaningful for non-deceased records. No duplicate rows, duplicate IDs, or out-of-range measurements were found.

Still unresolved

The source, collection method, sharing license, measurement units, and precise meaning/timing of Outcome are undocumented or inferred. The dataset’s real-world provenance has not been verified.

How to interpret this

The model is an exploratory baseline with uneven performance, particularly for Under Treatment. It is not a patient risk score or an estimate of survival or treatment effectiveness.

Project assessment: suitable as an educational end-to-end prototype. It is not ready for clinical use or real-world claims. Verify provenance and rights, define the outcome timing, and validate on an independent dataset before making stronger claims.
